## R1. E9: a manual sync taken over, and a fix found live

- **First run** (`eb193edb5`):
  - hub-a's lease last renewed at 14:18:22. hub-b claimed at **14:19:56**,
    34s after expiry, with the initiator kept (`by=lab-manual-sync`) and one
    hook Job.
  - hub-b's refreshes (`e9-first/hub-b-refreshes.txt`) show why the
    takeover waited. Its only full refresh during the operation was at
    14:18:20, the moment hub-a took the lease and **before hub-a's first
    mirror**.
  - `ClaimOrphan` looked at the mirror first, saw nothing running, and
    returned "none", so nothing was scheduled. The claim came from the
    periodic refresh at 14:19:55.
- **Fix** (`c8a0f15f5` → `10a35eef4`): a live lease held by another hub
  means "look again at expiry", whatever the mirror says.
- **Re-run** (`10a35eef4`):

```
14:38:07 manual sync requested on hub-a; lease held by hub-a
14:38:10 hub-a's last renewal          (killed 14:38:14)
14:39:15 hub-b: takeover, generation 32, "resuming it from waiting for completion of hook ... started 14:38:07"
14:40:30 hub-b: Succeeded, by=lab-manual-sync; 1 hook Job (created 14:38:08)
```

  hub-b's refreshes (`e9/hub-b-refreshes.txt`) were at 14:38:08 (full), two
  at level 0, then **14:39:15** (the scheduled one), 5s after expiry. The
  extra seconds past the 1s slack come from the refresh queue. The app was
  Synced on hub-b throughout, so nothing else would have made it refresh.

**hub-a returns** (gap 4): `finishedAt=2026-11-20T14:20:42Z` on both hubs,
for the first run's operation. In the C3 note, the returning hub showed the
time it came back.

## R2. E10: death in the sync phase, mid-rollout

hub-a's sync passed the PreSync hook and was waiting in wave 0 for the
Deployment, whose new pod turns Ready after 120s. hub-a was killed at
14:43:50.

```
--- 14:43:58

rs lab-pause-5d96f994c4 1 1 1      (old)
rs lab-pause-c88d77bd7 1 1 0       (new, not ready)
spoke generation=18                (wave 1 not applied yet)
--- 14:44:52
hub-b Running startedAt=14:43:25 by=auto info=multihub=taken over from hub-a | waiting for healthy state of apps/Deployment/lab-pause
--- 14:45:49
hub-b Succeeded startedAt=14:43:25 finishedAt=14:45:45
lab-presync-bd5e726-presync-1795185805   14:43:25   1   (one hook run)
rs lab-pause-5d96f994c4 0 0 0
rs lab-pause-c88d77bd7 1 1 1      (the same rollout finished)
spoke generation=19                (wave 1 applied by hub-b)
```

- **Claim:** 3s after expiry (renewal at 14:43:44, claim at 14:44:48).
- **Same rollout:** no third ReplicaSet. hub-b waited on the rollout hub-a
  had started.
- **Hook:** not re-run. The PreSync phase was already in the mirrored
  results.
- **Wave order kept:** the ConfigMap (wave 1) changed only after the
  Deployment was healthy, at 14:45:45.
- **hub-a returned** at 14:46 and followed (`finishedAt` 14:45:45 on both
  hubs).

## Remaining

- **Gaps 3 and 5 aren't verified live.**
- **Mirror size:** a large app could still hit the 200KiB cap, and its
  takeover would then restart the operation (a `mirror_error` warning
  beforehand).
- **Refresh cost:** while another hub runs an operation, each hub schedules
  about one refresh per lease duration for that app.
